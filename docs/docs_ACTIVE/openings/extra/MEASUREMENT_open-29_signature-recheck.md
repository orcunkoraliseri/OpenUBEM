## A method fact that changes two of the eight verdicts

`open09_fleet_err_taxonomy.py:38` — the regex that builds the family table:

```python
_MSG = re.compile(r"\*\*\s*(Warning|Severe)\s*\*\*\s*(.{0,90})")
```

**This matches only `Warning` and `Severe`. It never matches `Fatal`.** The committed taxonomy CSV
is structurally blind to every `** Fatal **` line in the corpus — not because none exist, but
because the family-counting regex cannot see the word. Confirmed by direct search of all 8,160 raw
`.err` files: `** Fatal **` appears in exactly 6 files, all six the already-known OPEN-42/OPEN-56
Warehouse placeholder buildings (`la_rural/way_472960972`, `la_rural/way_472961034`,
`la_rural/way_472961088`, `la_rural/way_472961091`, `la_rural/way_472961171`,
`la_urban/way_402215469`) — and the family CSV shows none of them, because none of their Fatal
lines can match `_MSG`. This matters directly for E-LA-15 and E-LA-17, both of whose defining
signatures are Fatal-severity.
