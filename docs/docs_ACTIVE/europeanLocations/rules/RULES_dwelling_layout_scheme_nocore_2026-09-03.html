D‑EU‑36

What survives from the original ruling

Ruled 2026‑08‑28  Coverage bar: ≥ 95 % of buildings is enough. 100 % is not required.

This half of the ruling is about how many buildings a ruled partitioner can express, not about what sits between the flats it draws, so the no‑core regime leaves it untouched. A building the ruled scheme cannot express is a FAIL, disclosed in the residual — never a silently reduced dwelling count.

🔴 The other half of the original ruling — whether a shared, unheated allowance is carved from the observed plate or added outside it — is moot under the no‑core regime: there is no such allowance to place either way (D-EU-79).

D‑EU‑79

The no-core regime, and what replaces the eight drawings

A plate divides into dwellings only, with nothing else drawn on it, and no rule, check or sheet here names what used to sit between the flats (D-EU-79). The method of record is scripts/eu21/07_nocore_tests.py: build_flats builds every candidate a plate’s shape allows — the default k equal-area column cut, every rows × cols grid on both axes (D-EU-83), a depth-layered re-cut of a thick band (D-EU-86), and, where the shape allows it, a ring-radial or per-wing cut — and keeps the best-scoring one. Every square metre left over by the cut is folded into the flat it touches most (D-EU-80), no part of a flat may be narrower than 2.00 m because of how the plate was divided (D-EU-81, restated D-EU-87), and no flat may be a ribbon (D-EU-82): every flat’s minimum rotated bounding rectangle must satisfy long / short ≤ MAX_FLAT_ASPECT.

Seven checks score every plate, five imported read-only from scripts/eu21/04_group_tests.py:312 (run_checks) and two added by this arc: C1 coverage 0.999 ≤ cov ≤ 1.001; C3 drawn flats equal claimed dwellings; C4 one room per flat, largest pairwise overlap < 0.02 m²; C5 simple outline, ≤ 40 points per zone; C6 outer-facade contact ≥ 2.50 m; C10 sum over flats of created-pinch area ≤ 0.10 m² (narrowness the footprint already had is excluded, narrowness the cut introduces is not, D-EU-87); C11 minimum rotated rectangle long/short ≤ MAX_FLAT_ASPECT (D-EU-82). There is no refusal path: every plate with a usable footprint is cut, and a plate that cannot clear a check is a FAIL, reported honestly.

Calibration. MAX_FLAT_ASPECT is set to the strictest of 2.5 / 3.0 / 3.5 / 4.0 that reaches FAIL 0 over all 550 plates (D-EU-84). After two genuine cutter-repair rounds no rung reached it; per D-EU-89 clause 2 the constant is set to the strictest rung, 2.5, marked not calibrated, and every residual plate ships as an honest FAIL (FINDING 238–243).
