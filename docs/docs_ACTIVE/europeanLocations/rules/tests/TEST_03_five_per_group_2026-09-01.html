CHECKS

The eight checks, C1–C8

  • C1 Coverage — flats + circulation = footprint, 100 % — no square metre left unassigned. Tolerance 0.999 ≤ cov ≤ 1.001. Source: groups sheet step 6 “100 %”; D-EU-64 §2.3 “≥ 99.9 %”; EXAMPLE §6.2 “area error 0.00 %”; MVP §4.3 area conservation.
  • C2 One circulation zone — exactly one circulation zone on the plate (Sliver: none). Tolerance n_core == 1 (Sliver: n_core ≤ 1). Source: D-EU-64 §2.1, §2.5; groups sheet step 6; EXAMPLE §6.3.
  • C3 Drawn = claimed — the drawn flat count equals the claimed flat count. Tolerance len(flats) == n. Source: D-EU-64 §2.4; groups sheet step 6.
  • C4 Each flat one room, no overlap — each flat is one connected room, no two zones overlap, and no zone carries a spike into another. Tolerance lobes_of(f) is None for every flat; largest overlap < 0.02 m². Source: D-EU-64 §2.6 (0.75 m erosion test); groups sheet step 6.
  • C5 Simple outline — every zone is a simple outline — no hole, nothing enclosed, 4 to 21 points — so each one writes straight out as a single floor surface. Tolerance no interior ring; no zone contains another; ≤ 40 points per zone. Source: D-EU-64 §2.10; groups sheet step 6 “4 to 21 points” (40 is a ceiling with headroom).
  • C6 Facade contact — every flat holds at least 2.50 m of the outer wall. Source: MVP §4.4 habitability gate; EXAMPLE §6.4 “smallest facade contact ≥ 2.50 m”; engine minimum_facade_contact_m = 2.5.
  • C7 Circulation share (report only, never pass/fail) — shown as x.x %; chip info inside 6–12 %, warn outside. Source: MVP §4.3 “6–12 % of gross floor area”; FINDING 204 (the 12–25 m² band conflicts and is unruled — report, not resolve); Corridor rectangle and Slab run above the band by construction (1.80 m corridor).
  • C8 Access (report only, never pass/fail) — the shortest gap between any flat and the circulation zone, minflats sbuf(f,0.05).intersection(circ).length; shown as x.xx m, n/a when the plate has no circulation (Sliver). Chip info when ≥ ACCESS_MIN_M (1.00 m) or n/a, warn otherwise. Source: PLAN_eu21-direct-cutters-2026-09-01.md §4 (ACCESS_MIN_M) and T04 §6 step 3.
SELECTION

How the buildings for this test were chosen

  • Universe: every usable census building with 2 to 8 declared flats per floor (its own count).
  • Sorted by declared plate area; 5 are taken at evenly spaced quantiles of that order — fewer usable candidates than 5 draws all of them.
  • Each is drawn at its own flats-per-floor count, not the group median.

A refused or failing plate is shown and counted here, never replaced by another building.
