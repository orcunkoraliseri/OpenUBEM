CHECKS

The ten checks, C1–C10

  • C1 Coverage — flats + circulation = footprint, 100 % — no square metre left unassigned. Tolerance 0.999 ≤ cov ≤ 1.001. Source: groups sheet step 6 “100 %”; D-EU-64 §2.3 “≥ 99.9 %”; EXAMPLE §6.2 “area error 0.00 %”; MVP §4.3 area conservation.
  • C2 One circulation zone — exactly one circulation zone on the plate (Sliver: none). Tolerance n_core == 1 (Sliver: n_core ≤ 1). Source: D-EU-64 §2.1, §2.5; groups sheet step 6; EXAMPLE §6.3.
  • C3 Drawn = claimed — the drawn flat count equals the claimed flat count. Tolerance len(flats) == n. Source: D-EU-64 §2.4; groups sheet step 6.
  • C4 Each flat one room, no overlap — each flat is one connected room, no two zones overlap, and no zone carries a spike into another. Tolerance lobes_of(f) is None for every flat; largest overlap < 0.02 m² (badge shows “n lobed” when a flat is not a single room, instead of the overlap area). Source: D-EU-64 §2.6 (0.75 m erosion test); groups sheet step 6; FINDING 232.
  • C5 Simple outline — every zone is a simple outline — no hole, nothing enclosed, 4 to 21 points — so each one writes straight out as a single floor surface. Tolerance no interior ring; no zone contains another; ≤ 40 points per zone. Source: D-EU-64 §2.10; groups sheet step 6 “4 to 21 points” (40 is a ceiling with headroom).
  • C6 Facade contact — every flat holds at least 2.50 m of contact with the plate’s outer perimeter (footprint.exterior only) — a courtyard, light-well or other interior void does not count towards it. Source: D-EU-69; MVP §4.4 habitability gate; EXAMPLE §6.4 “smallest facade contact ≥ 2.50 m”; engine minimum_facade_contact_m = 2.5.
  • C7 Circulation share (report only, never pass/fail) — shown as x.x %; chip info inside 6–12 %, warn outside. Source: MVP §4.3 “6–12 % of gross floor area”; FINDING 204 (the 12–25 m² band conflicts and is unruled — report, not resolve); Corridor rectangle and Slab run above the band by construction (1.80 m corridor).
  • C8 Access (hard check, enters the verdict) — the shortest length of corridor boundary lying within 1 cm of a flat, minflats sbuf(f,0.01).intersection(circ.exterior).length; shown as x.xx m, n/a when the plate has no circulation (Sliver). Passes when ≥ ACCESS_MIN_M (1.00 m), or n/a (no circulation zone — Sliver). Source: D-EU-70; FINDING 231 (the prior measure buffered the flat polygon by 5 cm and intersected the corridor as a Polygon, so .length returned the corridor’s perimeter across a bridged gap, not a real shared edge).
  • C9 Circulation simplicity (hard check, enters the verdict) — corner points of the single circulation zone, len(circ.exterior.coords) - 1; shown as an integer, n/a when the plate has no circulation (Sliver). Passes when ≤ CORRIDOR_MAX_POINTS (16), or n/a (no circulation zone — Sliver). Source: D-EU-71; threshold fixed by the owner at CP-2, 2026-09-02.
  • C10 Narrowest zone (hard check, enters the verdict) — over every zone (flats and circulation), the largest w in {0.5…4.0} m for which zone.buffer(-w/2) is non-empty, minimum taken over the zones; shown as x.x m, always computed. Passes when ≥ ZONE_MIN_WIDTH_M (2.00 m). Source: D-EU-72; threshold fixed by the owner at CP-2, 2026-09-02.
  • R2 is a reading, not a check — shown as a chip, never entering the verdict.
  • R2 Effective corridor width — 2 × circ.area / circ.length in metres, to 2 dp, against CORRIDOR_W = 1.80 m; n/a when the plate has no circulation. Source: D-EU-71; threshold not yet fixed.
SELECTION

How the buildings for this test were chosen

  • Universe: every usable census building, any own flats-per-floor count.
  • Per group and size (3, 6, 9, 12 flats per floor), 3 buildings whose declared plate area is nearest n × 60 m² are taken, skipping a building already used by a smaller size in the same group.
  • Each is drawn at the size’s imposed flats-per-floor count, not its own.

A refused or failing plate is shown and counted here, never replaced by another building.
