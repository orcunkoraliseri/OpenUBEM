CHECKS

The six checks, C1, C3, C4, C5, C6, C10

  • C1 Coverage — flats alone = 100 % of the footprint, D-EU-80 — no square metre left unassigned. Tolerance 0.999 ≤ cov ≤ 1.001.
  • C3 Drawn = claimed — the drawn flat count equals the claimed flat count. Tolerance len(flats) == n.
  • C4 Each flat one room, no overlap — each flat is one connected room, no two flats overlap. Tolerance lobes_of(f) is None for every flat; largest overlap < 0.02 m².
  • C5 Simple outline — every flat is a simple outline so it writes straight out as a single floor surface. Tolerance no interior ring; no zone contains another; ≤ 40 points per zone.
  • C6 Facade contact — every flat holds at least 2.50 m of contact with the plate's outer perimeter. Tolerance min ≥ 2.50 m against footprint.exterior only.
  • C10 Narrowest flat — no flat may contain any part narrower than 2.00 m, D-EU-81. Tolerance min widest_fit(f) ≥ 2.00 m over flats only.
  • C11 Flat proportion — no flat is a ribbon — a flat is at most 4 times as long as it is wide, D-EU-82. Tolerance max long/short of the minimum rotated rectangle ≤ 4.0.
RULES

The three laws of the dwellings-only regime

  • D-EU-79 — dwellings only: at this stage a plate is divided into dwellings only. Nothing but flats is drawn, and no rule, check or sheet refers to anything else.
  • D-EU-80 — no empty space per floor: every square metre of the floor plate belongs to exactly one flat. Any area left over by the division is absorbed into the flat it touches most, never left blank. Equal flat areas are not required and never justify leaving a gap.
  • D-EU-81 — nothing narrower than 2 m: no flat may contain any part narrower than 2.00 m. The width of a flat is the widest disc that fits inside it, measured exactly as C10 measures it.
SELECTION

How the buildings for this test were chosen

  • Universe: every usable census building with 2 to 8 declared flats per floor (its own count).
  • Sorted by declared plate area; 10 are taken at evenly spaced quantiles of that order.
  • Each is drawn at its own flats-per-floor count.
