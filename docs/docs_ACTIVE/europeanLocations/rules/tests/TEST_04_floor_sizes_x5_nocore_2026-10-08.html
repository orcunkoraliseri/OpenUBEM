CHECKS

The seven checks, C1, C3, C4, C5, C6, C10, C11

  • C1 Coverage — flats alone = 100 % of the footprint, D-EU-80 — no square metre left unassigned. Tolerance 0.999 ≤ cov ≤ 1.001.
  • C3 Drawn = claimed — the drawn flat count equals the claimed flat count. Tolerance len(flats) == n.
  • C4 Each flat one room, no overlap — each flat is one connected room, no two flats overlap. Tolerance lobes_of(f) is None for every flat; largest overlap < 0.02 m².
  • C5 Simple outline — every flat is a simple outline so it writes straight out as a single floor surface. Tolerance no interior ring; no zone contains another; ≤ 40 points per zone.
  • C6 Facade contact — every flat holds at least 2.50 m of contact with the plate's outer perimeter. Tolerance min ≥ 2.50 m against footprint.exterior only.
  • C10 Created pinch — no part of a flat may be narrower than 2.00 m because of how the plate was CUT — narrowness inherited from the footprint's own tips and slots is excluded; measured as a mitre-joined morphological opening at r = 1.00 m, D-EU-81/D-EU-87. Tolerance sum over flats of created-pinch area ≤ 0.10 m².
  • C11 Flat proportion — no flat is a ribbon — a flat is at most 5 times as long as it is wide, D-EU-82; waived when the plate is undivided (k = 1, the single flat is the plate itself), D-EU-93. Tolerance max long/short of the minimum rotated rectangle ≤ 5.0.
RULES

The three laws of the dwellings-only regime

  • D-EU-79 — dwellings only: at this stage a plate is divided into dwellings only. Nothing but flats is drawn, and no rule, check or sheet refers to anything else.
  • D-EU-80 — no empty space per floor: every square metre of the floor plate belongs to exactly one flat. Any area left over by the division is absorbed into the flat it touches most, never left blank. Equal flat areas are not required and never justify leaving a gap.
  • D-EU-81/D-EU-87 — nothing narrower than 2 m: no part of a flat may be narrower than 2.00 m because of how the plate was cut. Narrowness inherited from the footprint's own acute tips or slots is excluded — no cut can remove it; narrowness the cut itself creates is not, measured as a mitre-joined morphological opening at r = 1.00 m, exactly as C10 measures it.
SELECTION

How the buildings for this test were chosen

  • Universe: every usable census building, any own flats-per-floor count.
  • Per group and size (3, 6, 9, 12 flats per floor), 5 buildings whose declared plate area is nearest n × 60 m² are taken.
  • Each is drawn at the size’s imposed flats-per-floor count.
