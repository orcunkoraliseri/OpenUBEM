Bologna — Galvani 2 — result data

Files behind eu_IT-BOL-GALVANI2_viewer.html. Every number in the viewer comes from one of these; sources.json carries the sha256 of each input artefact.

Geometry for 1,220 residential buildings, read from the emitted IDFs (EU-11 win_2026-10-06 tree only). 1,164 were actually simulated (have an EUI, matching summary.json's population_run), 15 have an IDF but were never run, 41 have no IDF in the tree and are shown grey. Of the 1,179 with an IDF, 1,167 carry a dwelling layout ruled from their own IDF zones and 12 are a massing box (one undivided zone per storey). Zone polygons drawn in the floor-plan modal come directly from these IDFs, never from the layout side-cars.
