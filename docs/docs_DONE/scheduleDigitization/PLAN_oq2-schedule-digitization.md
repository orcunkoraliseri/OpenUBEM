## 1. Problem statement (one paragraph)

`openubem/data/schedules/doe_schedules.json` was shipped as a **synthetic placeholder**, not the real DOE prototype schedules. In `scripts/build_schedules_json.py`, only the **occupancy** day-profiles were hand-digitized; **lighting** and **equipment** are derived from occupancy by a linear transform — `lighting = max(0.1, 0.1 + 0.9·occ)`, `equipment = max(0.15, 0.15 + 0.85·occ)`. This breaks for any archetype whose occupancy never reaches zero. The **Apartment** group occupancy floor is 0.3 (people are home overnight), so its lighting is pinned at 0.37–0.91 all day → **5,831 equivalent full-load hours/yr** → at LPD 7.53 W/m² exactly the **43.9 kWh/m²** lighting EUI we observe. Real residential lighting runs ~1,500–2,500 EFLH. This systematically inflates internal-load end-uses and is the second root cause (after the zoning defect) of the V17 multifamily/LA overshoot. **DESIGN §3F OQ-2 already specifies the fix and its source:** one-time digitization of the 30-archetype × 6-family library from the **DOE Commercial Prototype schedule sets (NREL/openstudio-standards / PNNL prototype IDFs)**. This plan closes OQ-2 for the three **internal-load** families (occupancy, lighting, equipment); the **setpoint** (heating/cooling) and **infiltration** families are derived from scalar columns and stay as-is (DESIGN F16 invariant — do not touch).
