# DR04 — ML Surrogates, Emulators & Foundation Models for Building Energy Simulation

**Target:** OpenUBEM Phase 4 (optional ML emulator tier)  
**Date:** 2026-11-22

---

## Executive Summary

1. **No general-purpose "EnergyPlus foundation model" exists for UBEM design/retrofit screening.** NREL's **BuildingsBench** (`Buildings-900K`, CC-BY-4.0 data / BSD-3-Clause code) and **ResCast-100K** (CC-BY-4.0) are **autoregressive short-term load forecasting (STLF)** benchmarks requiring 4–7 days of historical meter values as input, not static physics-to-load emulators. Neither accepts envelope U-values, WWR, HVAC family retrofits, or 20 m context shading geometry ([Emami et al., 2023/2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.00142); [Dhruva et al., 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02852)).
2. **Pretrained STLF foundation models fail zero-shot on residential buildings and struggle with domain shifts.** In BuildingsBench, zero-shot `Transformer-L (Gaussian)` achieves 13.31% median NRMSE on real commercial buildings (beating Persistence Ensemble's 16.68%) but **79.34% NRMSE** on real residential buildings (worse than Persistence Ensemble's 77.88%). In ResCast-100K cross-domain leave-one-split-out tests across U.S. states, climate zones, walls, and heating systems, zero-shot total-load NRMSE stays at **42.9%–50.6%** (`TSMixer-R` / `Transformer-R`) and degrades to **75.6%–205.8%** zero-shot on real smart-meter data ([Emami et al., 2023/2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.00142); [Dhruva et al., 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02852)).
3. **1,000×–100,000× speedups are real for narrow, in-distribution parametric screening—not free across OOD districts.** Batched ML surrogates (GBDTs, MLPs, TCNs, DeepONets) routinely deliver sub-millisecond to few-millisecond amortized inference per building-year on GPU/CPU versus seconds-to-minutes for EnergyPlus (e.g., 426.8 s for 48 EnergyPlus runs vs 3.27 s for surrogate evaluation in [Zhangchaokai1, 2026](https://github.com/Zhangchaokai1/Energyplus-HcDeeponet-Retrofit); >100×–300× and order-of-magnitude speedups in NREL stock surrogates [Zhang et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scs.2021.103292); [Bhavsar et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enbuild.2023.113395)).
4. **In-distribution accuracy is strong, but OOD shifts (unseen climates, heating systems, unusual geometry, Pareto slopes) break black-box ML.** In-distribution annual EUI/total energy surrogates often achieve $R^2 \approx 0.91\text{–}0.997$ and MAPE $\approx 0.7\%\text{–}8\%$, and hourly multi-climate TCNs report $<3\%$ annual heating deviation on unseen designs/locations ([Westermann et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2020.115563)). However, [Moutaouakil et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1080/19401493.2026.2762498) show that XGBoost with $R^2 = 0.997$ still exhibits **39.51% mean HAMT-referenced MVR** (physical/monotonicity violations), and [Zhangchaokai1 (2026)](https://github.com/Zhangchaokai1/Energyplus-HcDeeponet-Retrofit) shows that even at **0.83% annual MAPE**, **8 of 48 (16.7%)** surrogate-optimal Pareto schemes fail non-dominance upon EnergyPlus re-simulation.
5. **NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo (formerly Modulus) has no EnergyPlus/UBEM building modules.** Inspection of `NVIDIA/physicsnemo` (`Apache-2.0`) shows examples for CFD, weather, and structural PDEs, with zero EnergyPlus or lumped building thermal surrogates. Conversely, **PNNL NeuroMANCER** (`pnnl/neuromancer`, Battelle permissive license) provides working PyTorch Neural-ODE / RC-network / state-space building thermal modules ([Drgoňa et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enbuild.2021.110992)).
6. **Sample efficiency favors multi-fidelity physics + residual ML over pure black-box deep learning.** Pure tabular surrogates need ~$10d\text{–}20d$ Latin Hypercube / Sobol samples per archetype-climate slice (hundreds to low thousands of E+ runs; [Westermann & Evins, 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enbuild.2019.05.057); [Zhangchaokai1, 2026](https://github.com/Zhangchaokai1/Energyplus-HcDeeponet-Retrofit) reaches 0.48% validation MAPE by $N=100$ for 9 retrofit parameters on one building). Microclimate/multi-archetype UBEMs scale better via prototype-wise adaptive sampling ([Pan et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1061/9780784486139.051)) or physics-constrained residual learning on top of a fast RC/ISO 52016 backbone ([Di Natale et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2022.119806)).
7. **Trust requires calibrated uncertainty + hard OOD gating back to EnergyPlus.** Deep ensembles (5 members) combined with **Split Conformal Prediction** (e.g., via `MAPIE`, BSD-3-Clause) provide finite-sample coverage guarantees without Gaussian error assumptions ([Borrotti, 2024](https://doi.org/10.3390/en17174348); [FLUME, arXiv:2503.19708](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19708)), while Mahalanobis/leverage OOD detectors and ensemble spread route uncertain or unusual buildings to EnergyPlus 23.1.
8. **Clear Recommendation for OpenUBEM:** **Do NOT fine-tune BuildingsBench.** Instead, build a **two-track, strictly opt-in emulator trained on OpenUBEM's existing tens of thousands of EnergyPlus 23.1 runs**:
   - **Track A (Immediate, Phase 4a):** **Hybrid Physics-Residual LightGBM/XGBoost Ensemble + Split Conformal Prediction (`MAPIE`)** predicting annual/monthly end-uses by fuel (using a GPU-batched ISO 52016 / RC baseline from DR03 as the physics prior, or pure tabular features when DR03 is absent), gated by feature-space OOD checks + EnergyPlus verification of top Pareto/screening candidates.
   - **Track B (Phase 4b, hourly loads):** **Multi-Fidelity Weather-Guided Residual TCN / TSMixer-style sequence model** predicting the *residual* between a fast RC model and EnergyPlus hourly meters, keeping physical bounds (non-negativity, temperature monotonicity, energy balance) intact.
9. **Honest "Pure Physics" Positioning:** Never market ML outputs as "EnergyPlus-equivalent physics." Label every emulator output `tier="ml_surrogate_screen"`, attach calibrated conformal intervals `[y_low, y_high]`, log the exact fraction of buildings resolved by E+ fallback, and enforce mandatory EnergyPlus 23.1 re-simulation for final reporting, code compliance, and top-ranked retrofit/Pareto candidates.

## Q1 & Q2: State of the Art and Accuracy Evidence (In-Distribution vs. OOD)

### 1. Foundation & Pretrained Building Models (`BuildingsBench`, `ResCast-100K`)
A critical distinction often missed in UBEM literature reviews is **Short-Term Load Forecasting (STLF)** vs. **Design/Retrofit Surrogate Emulation**:
- **NREL BuildingsBench / Buildings-900K** ([Emami et al., NeurIPS 2023, arXiv:2307.00142](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.00142)): Extracts 900K hourly total-energy time series (550K ResStock + 350K ComStock from NREL's 2021 EULP database; ~110 GB Parquet, 2018 AMY + TMY) and benchmarks against >1,900 real buildings from 7 open datasets. Input covariates are **only** past 168 h load, cyclic calendar features, PUMA lat/lon centroid, and a binary commercial/residential flag—**no envelope U-values, HVAC type, WWR, geometry, or weather channels**.
  - *Zero-shot OOD (sim-to-real):* `Transformer-L (Gaussian)` (160.7M params) reaches **13.31% median NRMSE** on real commercial buildings (vs 16.68% Persistence Ensemble), but **79.34% NRMSE** on real residential buildings (worse than Persistence Ensemble's 77.88%). On held-out simulated Buildings-900K PUMAs, NRMSE is 15.10% (commercial) and 43.52% (residential).
  - *Fine-tuned (6 months real data):* `Transformer-L (Gaussian)` improves slightly to **12.96% NRMSE** (commercial) and **77.20% NRMSE** (residential), beating LightGBM (16.02% / 80.07%), at the cost of 1.5–3.5 GPU-hours on a 40 GB A100 to fine-tune just 200 buildings (~15+ hours for the full benchmark).
  - *Verdict for OpenUBEM:* Unusable as a design/retrofit/climate-scenario emulator because it cannot respond to physical parameter changes without 6 months of metered history.
- **ResCast-100K** ([Dhruva et al., NeurIPS 2026 / arXiv:2606.02852](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02852)): 100K ResStock EnergyPlus homes at 15-min resolution with coupled targets (`total_load`, `hvac_load`, `indoor_temp`), weather, HVAC setpoints, and >40 static covariates (still omitting explicit wall U-values/insulation values, flagged by the authors as future work).
  - *In-domain (4-day context → 1-day forecast):* `TimeXer` achieves 40.43% NRMSE (total), 32.56% (HVAC), 0.98% (temp); `TSMixer` achieves 42.08% / 35.94% / 1.19%.
  - *Cross-domain zero-shot (unseen State / Climate Zone / Wall / Heating split):* `TSMixer-R` leads at **42.89%–45.25% NRMSE** (total load) and **36.41%–38.86%** (HVAC), with **heating technology shifts producing the highest cross-domain variance** ($\pm 6.46\%$–$6.88\%$ for LSTM-R/Transformer-R).
  - *Sim-to-real zero-shot (ECOBEE, HEAPO, IDEAL, NEST, REFIT):* Total load NRMSE degrades to **75.6%–100.8%** (`TSMixer-R` best in 6/8 columns, `TimeXer-R` hits 205.8% on NEST total load), though probabilistic CRPS remains more stable.

### 2. ResStock / ComStock & DOE National Lab Surrogates
Note first that NREL's official **End-Use Savings Shapes (EUSS)** dataset was generated by running full EnergyPlus simulations via `buildstockbatch` on HPC (baseline + measure pairs across 550K ResStock / 350K ComStock models), **not** by replacing EnergyPlus with ML surrogates ([OSTI / NREL EUSS](https://www.osti.gov/)). However, DOE labs (NREL, ANL, LBNL, PNNL) have published several dedicated EnergyPlus stock surrogates:
- **NREL/ANL High-Resolution Hourly Stock Surrogate** ([Zhang et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scs.2021.103292)): 6-module framework (BuildStockBatch → Dask/Parquet ETL → ML surrogates) trained on ResStock/ComStock EnergyPlus runs. Commercial case study achieved **14.8% CV(RMSE)**, **7.5% peak load magnitude error**, **11.2% peak timing error**, and ~95% runtime reduction (`UNVERIFIED` exact GPU/CPU hardware breakdown behind Elsevier paywall; summary figures verified via OSTI record `1820582`).
- **NREL/VT LightGBM Residential End-Use Surrogate** ([Bhavsar et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enbuild.2023.113395)): LightGBM trained on ResStock EnergyPlus hourly profiles (tested on 100 diverse Cook County, IL homes) achieved a **53% RMSE reduction** over prior decision-tree surrogates, captured load spikes/ramp rates, and yielded **~10× runtime reduction** vs simulation.
- **NREL Commercial Benchmarking Surrogate** ([Chinde et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enbuild.2026.117033)): Polynomial ridge regression (MLR + interactions + ridge) trained on OpenStudio/EnergyPlus small office & retail strip mall prototypes across HVAC types and ASHRAE climate zones achieved **CV(RMSE) 1.06–3.26** (exact %/scale units `UNVERIFIED` behind paywall; OSTI `3017555` confirms ~14.9% error reduction from change-point/input transforms + SHAP interpretability).
- **LBNL/Georgia Tech UBEM Microclimate Sampling Study** ([Pan et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1061/9780784486139.051)): Quantifies prototype-wise surrogate sensitivity to microclimate sampling ratio in EnergyPlus UBEMs.
- **PNNL NeuroMANCER / Physics-Constrained Deep Learning** ([Drgoňa et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enbuild.2021.110992)): Encodes RC thermal network structure, Perron-Frobenius eigenvalue bounds (guaranteed dissipativeness/stability), and inequality penalties into recurrent neural state-space models; generalizes over 20 days from 10 days of 20-zone office training data.

### 3. Architecture Comparison (Annual vs. Hourly, In-Distribution vs. OOD)

- **GBDTs (XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost, ExtraTrees) on Annual/Monthly Features:** Consistently dominate small-to-medium tabular EnergyPlus datasets (500–10,000 runs) in training speed and accuracy ([Westermann & Evins, 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enbuild.2019.05.057); [Ali et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enbuild.2023.113768): 91% accuracy on 1M synthetic Irish residential models across 19 variables). **Weakness:** Zero extrapolation outside training bounds (tree leaf values are piecewise-constant outside min/max feature range) and poor sensitivity/slope preservation—e.g., [Moutaouakil et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1080/19401493.2026.2762498) found XGBoost scored $R^2=0.997$ in-distribution across 6 Moroccan climates yet failed Tier 3 physical consistency with **39.51% HAMT-referenced MVR**.
- **MLPs / ResNets (Annual & Hourly):** Smooth gradients and fast GPU batching. [Thrampoulidis et al. (2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2020.116024), [2023)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2022.120566) used ANNs on Swiss archetypes to approximate optimal retrofit packages ($R^2 = 0.91$, $F_1 = 0.89$); [Nutkiewicz et al. (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2018.05.023) (DUE-S) combined EnergyPlus + ResNet on urban blocks, reaching **11.4% monthly / 14.4% daily / 25.6% hourly CV(RMSE)** (test monthly MAPE **8.28%**). On 1 residential building's 9-parameter retrofit space, a simple `HC-NN` MLP got 2.18% annual total MAPE vs `ExtraTrees` 2.37%, while `Ridge` failed completely at 15.91% MAPE ($R^2 = -0.60$) ([Zhangchaokai1, 2026](https://github.com/Zhangchaokai1/Energyplus-HcDeeponet-Retrofit)).
- **Sequence Models (TCN, LSTM, TSMixer, Transformer):** Essential when hourly or sub-hourly thermal mass dynamics matter. [Westermann et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2020.115563) introduced a deep Temporal Convolutional Network (TCN) over hourly weather + building parameters, achieving **$<3\%$ error on annual heating demand at unseen climate locations** outside the training set. Similarly, [arXiv:2603.11121 (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11121) shows that weekly weather-guided representations trained on a *single* location transfer within the same climate zone with negligible loss and minimal cross-climate degradation.
- **Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) over Zones/Surfaces/Urban Blocks:** Naturally encode arbitrary zone counts, inter-zone partitions, and neighbor shading topologies without fixed-length vector padding. **PISTGNN** ([Lee & Cho, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enbuild.2024.115085)) reduced RMSE by **44.7%** over data-driven baselines and achieved **0.78 K** multi-zone temperature RMSE (vs 1.51 K LSTM, 1.48 K GRU). Urban graph surrogates ([Kim et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.buildenv.2026.114384); [IBPSA 2025](https://doi.org/10.26868/25222708.2025.1305)) model district-level load/3D morphology graphs, though open production-ready EnergyPlus-to-PyG converters do not yet exist.
- **Neural Operators (DeepONet, FNO) & PINNs (`PhysicsNeMo`, `PCNN`, `NeuroMANCER`):**
  - *DeepONet:* `HC-DeepONet` (branch net on 9 building/retrofit parameters, trunk net on 17 daily weather/calendar features) outperforms `HC-NN` and `ExtraTrees` on the same EnergyPlus data: **0.74% annual validation MAPE ($R^2=0.993$)** vs 2.18% (`HC-NN`) and 2.37% (`ExtraTrees`), and **0.83% MAPE ($R^2=0.996$)** across 48 Pareto-front candidates ([Zhangchaokai1, 2026](https://github.com/Zhangchaokai1/Energyplus-HcDeeponet-Retrofit)). Yet 8/48 Pareto schemes still changed dominance when checked in EnergyPlus.
  - *FNO:* Excels at 2D/3D spatial fields (e.g., FLUME urban wind/temp CFD emulator: 0.2 m/s and 0.19 °C MAE on unseen urban blocks from 23 CFD blocks, [arXiv:2503.19708](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19708); indoor ventilation CFD control, [arXiv:2504.21243](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21243)), but offers little advantage over TCN/DeepONet for 1D EnergyPlus zone loads and struggled on tabular hygrothermal checks in [Moutaouakil et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1080/19401493.2026.2762498).
  - *PINNs & Hybrid RC+ML:* Pure PDE PINNs (like `PhysicsNeMo`) are mismatched to EnergyPlus because EnergyPlus couples 1D conduction with discrete HVAC staging, schedules, and view-factor radiation—not a single closed PDE. **Physically Consistent Neural Networks (PCNNs)** ([Di Natale et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2022.119806), [2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2023.121071)) and **Hybrid RC + Residual ML** solve this by running an RC linear/nonlinear thermal backbone in parallel with a bounded NN residual, enforcing physical monotonicity (heating/cooling signs, temperature bounds) and cutting OOD drift dramatically.

### What Breaks Out-of-Distribution (OOD)?
1. **Unseen HVAC Families / Heating Fuel Shifts:** Highest error/variance across all cross-domain tests (e.g., ResCast-100K Heating split; switching gas boiler ↔ air-source heat pump changes nonlinear COP curves and part-load ratios that tabular interpolators cannot extrapolate).
2. **Extreme / Future Climates (Heatwaves, Unseen Climate Zones):** Tree surrogates clip at training temperature maxima; pure black-box NNs extrapolate cooling loads unphysically unless weather-guided sequence features ([Westermann et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2020.115563)) or physics RC priors ([Di Natale et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2022.119806)) are used.
3. **Unusual Geometry & Context Shading:** Fixed-vector surrogates trained on rectangular prototypes break on L/U-shaped footprints, multi-dwelling zone adjacency, or asymmetric 20 m urban canyon shading unless geometry is encoded via orientation-resolved areas and obstruction metrics (see Q3).
4. **Optimization & Ranking Slopes:** Even with $<1\%$ MAPE, local surrogate gradient/ranking errors flip Pareto dominance (~16.7% in [Zhangchaokai1, 2026](https://github.com/Zhangchaokai1/Energyplus-HcDeeponet-Retrofit)).

## Q3: Data Needs, Sampling Strategies, and Geometry/Shading Features

### 1. How Many EnergyPlus Runs Are Needed?
Required sample counts depend heavily on **parameter dimensionality ($d$)**, **target resolution (annual vs. hourly)**, and **whether a physics prior is present**:
- **Single-building parametric retrofit ($d \approx 8\text{–}15$):** Space-filling rules of thumb start at **$10d\text{–}20d$** runs ([Westermann & Evins, 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enbuild.2019.05.057)). Empirically, `HC-DeepONet` on 9 retrofit parameters ([Zhangchaokai1, 2026](https://github.com/Zhangchaokai1/Energyplus-HcDeeponet-Retrofit)) achieves:
  - $N = 50$: validation $R^2 = 0.9877 \pm 0.0023$, MAPE $= 0.72\% \pm 0.09\%$ (Pareto MAPE $1.26\%$)
  - $N = 100$: validation $R^2 = 0.9944 \pm 0.0003$, MAPE $= 0.48\% \pm 0.01\%$ (Pareto MAPE $0.75\%$)
  - $N = 150$: validation $R^2 = 0.9963 \pm 0.0009$, MAPE $= 0.38\% \pm 0.03\%$ (Pareto MAPE $0.71\%$)
- **Per archetype × climate cell in UBEM:** Pure black-box surrogates across 15–25 varying building/urban features typically need **500–2,000 E+ runs per archetype** when spanning multiple climates, whereas OpenUBEM's existing **tens of thousands of E+ runs** across archetypes, 10 HVAC families, and ERA5/TMYx climates already exceed the threshold for tabular annual/monthly training—*provided* underrepresented HVAC families and extreme climates are checked for coverage gaps.

### 2. Sampling Strategies
- **Latin Hypercube Sampling (LHS) & Sobol Sequences:** Maximin LHS and low-discrepancy Sobol sequences outperform random Monte Carlo for initial coverage. Standard LHS undersamples corners/extrema (e.g., high insulation + high WWR + low SHGC), so **boundary-augmented LHS** (explicitly adding extreme corners) prevents extrapolation cliffs ([Westermann & Evins, 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enbuild.2019.05.057)).
- **Prototype-Wise Adaptive & Active Learning:** Instead of uniform sampling across all archetypes, [Pan et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1061/9780784486139.051) show surrogate sensitivity varies strongly by prototype, justifying prototype-specific sampling ratios. Active learning (e.g., LOLA-Voronoi in BESOS, or **ensemble disagreement / conformal width acquisition**) focuses new E+ runs on high-variance boundaries and HVAC regime transitions.
- **Multi-Fidelity (Cheap ROM + Few E+ Runs):** Combining a fast reduced-order physics model (e.g., GPU-batched ISO 52016 / 5R1C from DR03) with sparse E+ runs—as in DOE's multi-fidelity optimization work ([OSTI 3014930](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/3014930)) and residual learning—reduces required E+ training runs by ~3×–10× because the ML model only learns the *residual* $\Delta = y_{\text{E+}} - y_{\text{RC}}$ rather than rediscovering solar geometry and heat conduction from scratch.

### 3. Geometry & Context-Shading Feature Engineering (for OpenUBEM)
Because OpenUBEM models real footprints, dwelling-level zones, and **20 m context shading**, feeding raw polygons or only footprint area + height loses critical physics. Effective fixed-length tabular/sequence encodings from literature ([Nutkiewicz et al., 2018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2018.05.023); [arXiv:2503.19708](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19708); UBEM feature studies) include:
- **Orientation-resolved envelope areas:** Exterior wall area and glazed area ($A_{\text{wall}, d}, A_{\text{win}, d}$) binned into **8 compass sectors** (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW), plus total roof area, ground-contact floor area, conditioned volume $V$, and **surface-to-volume ratio ($S/V$)** / compactness.
- **Zoning & adjacency features:** Dwelling/zone count, mean zone floor area, shared adiabatic party-wall fraction (row/mid-terrace vs detached).
- **20 m Context Shading & Visibility Encodings:**
  - Facade-weighted **Sky View Factor ($\text{SVF}$)** and **Solar Obstruction Angle** across 8 or 16 azimuth bins (analogous to FLUME's Multi-Directional Distance Feature, MDDF, [arXiv:2503.19708](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19708)).
  - **Shaded-to-unshaded incident solar ratio** per facade orientation (computable analytically or via GPU raytracing in DR06 in milliseconds *without* running E+!).
  - Height-to-distance ratios ($H_{\text{neighbor}} / d_{\text{street}}$) to nearest buildings within the 20 m radius.

Precomputing district geometry/shading features in vectorized batches (or GPU raytracing from DR06) is mandatory.

## Q5: Uncertainty Quantification, OOD Detection & E+ Fallback

To prevent silent surrogate hallucinations from corrupting district decisions:

1. **Epistemic Uncertainty via Deep Ensembles ($K=5$):** Train 5 independent models (different random seeds + bootstrap/subsampled splits or different tree seeds). Ensemble standard deviation $\sigma_{\text{ens}}(x)$ captures epistemic uncertainty in sparse regions of parameter space ([Zhangchaokai1, 2026](https://github.com/Zhangchaokai1/Energyplus-HcDeeponet-Retrofit); [FLUME, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19708)). **Prefer 5-member Deep Ensembles over MC Dropout**—MC Dropout is often miscalibrated under covariate shift and disables deterministic batching optimizations.
2. **Calibrated Prediction Intervals via Split Conformal Prediction (CQR / Ensemble-Conformal):**
   - Hold out a calibration set $\mathcal{D}_{\text{cal}}$ stratified by archetype and climate (e.g., 1,000–2,000 E+ runs).
   - Compute normalized nonconformity scores $s_i = |y_i - \hat{\mu}(x_i)| / (\sigma_{\text{ens}}(x_i) + \epsilon)$ (or Conformalized Quantile Regression via `MAPIE`), yielding distribution-free intervals $\hat{y}(x) \pm \hat{q}_{1-\alpha}\,(\sigma_{\text{ens}}(x)+\epsilon)$ with guaranteed marginal coverage $1-\alpha$ under exchangeability ([Borrotti, 2024](https://doi.org/10.3390/en17174348); [FLUME, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19708)).
   - **Important caveat:** Coverage guarantees degrade under severe OOD shift—which is why conformal intervals must be paired with an explicit OOD detector.
3. **Multi-Gate OOD Detection → Mandatory EnergyPlus 23.1 Fallback:** Route a building-scenario pair $x$ directly to EnergyPlus 23.1 if **any** gate triggers:
   - **Gate 1 (Hard Domain Bounds / Categorical Match):** Unseen HVAC family, unseen archetype class, or continuous parameter outside `[min, max]` of training envelope (prevents GBDT clipping and NN wild extrapolation).
   - **Gate 2 (Feature-Space Distance):** Mahalanobis distance $D_M(x)$ (in standardized continuous feature space per archetype) or $k$-NN distance exceeding the 99th percentile of $\mathcal{D}_{\text{train}}$.
   - **Gate 3 (Relative Epistemic Uncertainty):** Conformal half-width $w_{90}(x) / \max(\hat{y}(x), \tau) > \delta_{\max}$ (e.g., $>15\%$ for annual EUI screening, or $>25\%$ for hourly peak demand).
   - **Gate 4 (Physical Sanity & Pareto Verification):** Negative loads, heating/cooling sign inversions, energy-balance residual violations, **plus mandatory E+ re-simulation of the top-ranked candidates (e.g., Pareto front)** to eliminate the ~16.7% dominance-flip failure mode observed in [Zhangchaokai1 (2026)](https://github.com/Zhangchaokai1/Energyplus-HcDeeponet-Retrofit).

## Q7: Proposed Pipeline for OpenUBEM

**Core Decision:** **Train our own Hybrid Physics-Residual + Ensemble Surrogate on OpenUBEM's EnergyPlus 23.1 archive** (NOT fine-tuning BuildingsBench, and NOT pure PDE PINNs).

```text
[GeoPandas District + ERA5/TMYx + Archetype/HVAC]
       │
       ├──► (1) Vectorized Feature Extractor (Python / Polars / GeoPandas)
       │        • 8-sector wall/window areas, S/V, WWR, zone count, party-wall ratio
       │        • 20m context shading: SVF, 8-bin obstruction angles, shaded solar ratios
       │        • HVAC family (one-hot/embed), COP/efficiency, setpoints, U-values
       │        • Climate summary (HDD/CDD, GHI, T_99%/T_1%) or hourly weather tensor
       │
       ├──► (2) Optional Physics Prior (DR03 GPU-Batched RC / ISO 52016)
       │        • Computes baseline physical load y_RC (always physically bounded)
       │
       ├──► (3) OOD Gate 1 & 2 (Pre-Inference)
       │        • Unseen HVAC/archetype, out-of-bounds parameter, or high Mahalanobis D_M
       │        └─► IF TRIGGERED ──► [EnergyPlus 23.1 Fallback Queue]
       │
       ├──► (4) ML Residual / Direct Surrogate Ensemble (K=5, GPU-Batched)
       │        • Phase 4a (Annual/Monthly by fuel): 5× LightGBM/XGBoost + Conformal (MAPIE)
       │        • Phase 4b (Hourly 8,760h): 5× Residual TCN / TSMixer over (weather + y_RC)
       │        • Predicts y_hat = y_RC + f_ML(x) (clamped ≥ 0) + calibrated [y_low, y_high]
       │
       ├──► (5) OOD Gate 3 & 4 (Post-Inference)
       │        • Relative conformal interval width > threshold OR physics violation
       │        • OR candidate selected in Top-K / Pareto Front
       │        └─► IF TRIGGERED ──► [EnergyPlus 23.1 Verification Run]
       │
       └──► (6) Provenance-Tagged Output
                • tier="ml_surrogate_screen" vs "energyplus_23_1_verified"
                • Per-building uncertainty interval + district fallback % audit log
```

### Step-by-Step Implementation Plan
1. **Data Extraction from Existing E+ Runs:** Parse OpenUBEM's tens of thousands of historical E+ 23.1 `eplusout.sql` / Parquet summaries into a canonical **training lake** indexed by `(building_hash, weather_hash, hvac_family, archetype_family)`. **Critical:** split train / calibration / OOD-test strictly by **grouped keys** (hold out entire climate years, entire districts, and held-out HVAC/retrofit combinations) — never random row splits across correlated runs.
2. **Phase 4a — Annual & Fuel-Disaggregated Screening Emulator:** Start with an ensemble of 5 **XGBoost/LightGBM** models (predicting annual heating, cooling, DHW, and baseload electricity/gas separately rather than total EUI, preventing cancellation of errors) wrapped in **Split Conformal Prediction (`MAPIE`)**. When DR03's GPU-batched RC/ISO 52016 model is available, predict the *residual* $y_{\text{E+}} - y_{\text{RC}}$ so physical climate/envelope trends extrapolate cleanly.
3. **Phase 4b — Hourly Load & Peak Screening Emulator:** Train a lightweight 5-member **Residual TCN or TSMixer** (`PyTorch`, ~1M–5M params) conditioned on hourly ERA5/TMYx weather + static building/shading vectors + hourly $y_{\text{RC}}(t)$ to predict 8,760-hour electricity/fuel residuals.
4. **EnergyPlus 23.1 Fallback Policy (Explicit SLA):**
   - **Automatic fallback** when any Gate 1–4 triggers.
   - **Mandatory verification** on all final Pareto-front / recommended retrofit packages (e.g., top 50 candidates, eliminating ranking-flip errors).
   - **Active learning loop:** Every fallback E+ 23.1 run is appended to the training lake for periodic offline recalibration.

## Risks & Keeping the "Pure Physics" Claim Honest

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Erosion of OpenUBEM's "pure physics, zero fitted multipliers" promise** | **Critical** | Make ML strictly **opt-in** (`--emulator` flag, default OFF). Never blend unlabelled ML numbers into EnergyPlus results. Every output object and UI/report badge must expose `simulation_engine` (`energyplus_23_1` vs `ml_surrogate_screen`), model version, conformal coverage level, and `%_fallback_to_eplus`. Ban terms like *"EnergyPlus-accurate AI"*; call it *"ML screening emulator with conformal bounds and EnergyPlus verification."* |
| **Silent OOD failure on unseen HVAC families, climates, or unusual geometry** | **High** | Enforce hard categorical/range guards + Mahalanobis OOD gating + ensemble conformal width triggers to route OOD buildings back to EnergyPlus 23.1. Never allow GBDTs to silently clip out-of-range climates. |
| **Pareto ranking / retrofit optimization reversals** | **High** | As shown empirically by [Zhangchaokai1 (2026)](https://github.com/Zhangchaokai1/Energyplus-HcDeeponet-Retrofit), <1% annual MAPE still caused 16.7% Pareto dominance errors. Require EnergyPlus 23.1 re-simulation of all shortlisted/Pareto-optimal candidates before finalizing decisions. |
| **Physical conservation & monotonicity violations** | **Medium–High** | Predict disaggregated end-uses, use RC residual formulation ($y_{\text{RC}} + f_{\text{ML}}$), enforce non-negativity, and run Tier 3 sensitivity/monotonicity audits ([Moutaouakil et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1080/19401493.2026.2762498)) on every released model checkpoint (e.g., better U-value or higher COP must not increase heating energy). |
| **Feature-extraction bottleneck negating GPU speedup** | **Medium** | Vectorize 20 m context shading (SVF, 8-bin obstruction) in NumPy/GeoPandas/GPU raytracing (DR06) so per-building preprocessing takes <0.5 ms rather than 50 ms in Python loops. |
| **Licence contamination** | **Low–Medium** | Avoid `GPL-3.0` (`besos`, `fastep`), `LGPL-2.1` (`deepxde`), and `ResStock` Clause 3 copyleft dependencies in OpenUBEM core. Build solely on `BSD-3-Clause` (`MAPIE`, `scikit-learn`), `MIT` (`LightGBM`, `PyTorch Geometric`), `Apache-2.0` (`XGBoost`), and `PyTorch` (`BSD-3-Clause`). |
