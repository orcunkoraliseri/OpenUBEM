London — St Dunstan's — result data

Files behind eu_GB-LDN-STDUNSTANS_viewer.html. Every number in the viewer comes from one of these; sources.json carries the sha256 of each input artefact.

Geometry for 1,242 residential buildings, read from the emitted IDFs (EU-17 rebuild tree). 17 carry a dwelling layout ruled from their own IDF zones, 65 are a massing box (one undivided zone per storey), 1,160 have no IDF in the EU-17 tree and are shown grey. Zone polygons drawn in the floor-plan modal come directly from these IDFs, never from the layout side-cars.
