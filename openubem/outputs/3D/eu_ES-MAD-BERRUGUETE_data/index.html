Madrid — Berruguete — result data

Files behind eu_ES-MAD-BERRUGUETE_viewer.html. Every number in the viewer comes from one of these; sources.json carries the sha256 of each input artefact.

Geometry for 1,194 residential buildings, read from the emitted IDFs (EU-11 ceiling82 tree first, EU-17 rebuild tree as fallback). 1,175 were actually simulated (have an EUI, matching summary.json's population_run), 6 have an IDF but were never run, 13 have no IDF in either tree and are shown grey. Of the 1,181 with an IDF, 1,038 carry a dwelling layout ruled from their own IDF zones and 143 are a massing box (one undivided zone per storey). Zone polygons drawn in the floor-plan modal come directly from these IDFs, never from the layout side-cars.
