Budapest — Erzsébetváros — result data

Files behind eu_HU-BUD-ERZSEBETVAROS_viewer.html. Every number in the viewer comes from one of these; sources.json carries the sha256 of each input artefact.

Geometry for 805 residential buildings, read from the simulated IDFs (yes tree; 48 homes left out because they could not be divided into flats correctly). 757 were actually simulated (have an EUI, matching summary.json's population_run), 0 have an IDF but were never run, 0 have no IDF in the tree and are shown grey. Of the 757 with an IDF, 805 carry a dwelling layout ruled from their own IDF zones and 0 are a massing box (one undivided zone per storey). Zone polygons drawn in the floor-plan modal come directly from these IDFs, never from the layout side-cars.
