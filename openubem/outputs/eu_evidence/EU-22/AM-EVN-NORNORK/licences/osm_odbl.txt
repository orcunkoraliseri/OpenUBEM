      Copyright and License
</h1>
        </div>

  <div lang="en" dir="ltr">
    <p class="lead">
      OpenStreetMap provides open map data for thousands of websites, mobile apps, and hardware devices.
OpenStreetMap is built by a community of people like you who contribute and maintain mapping data
about roads, trails, cafés, railway stations, and much more, all over the world.

    </p>
    <p class="lead">
      <a href="https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/About_OpenStreetMap">Learn more about OpenStreetMap</a> and <a href="/user/new">get started mapping</a>!
You can read more on the <a href="https://blog.openstreetmap.org/">OpenStreetMap Blog</a>, and subscribe to <a href="https://weeklyosm.eu/">weeklyOSM</a>.
Find other mappers in your local <a href="/communities">OSM community</a>.
Support the project by joining the <a href="https://osmfoundation.org/">OSM Foundation</a> and <a href="https://supporting.openstreetmap.org/donate/">making a donation</a>.

    </p>

    </p>

      You are free to copy, distribute, transmit and adapt our data,
as long as you credit OpenStreetMap and its
contributors. If you alter or build upon our data, you
may distribute the result only under the same license. The
<a href="https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/">full legal code at Open Data Commons</a> explains your rights and responsibilities.

    </p>

    </p>
    <p>
      Read more about using our data at the <a href="https://osmfoundation.org/Licence">OSMF Licence page</a>.

    </p>

    <h3>How to credit OpenStreetMap</h3>
    <p>
      Where you use OpenStreetMap data, you are required to do the following two things:

    </p>
    <ul>
      <li>Provide credit to OpenStreetMap by displaying our attribution notice.</li>
      <li>Make clear that the data is available under the Open Database License.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>
      For the attribution notice, we have different <a href="https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Attribution_Guidelines">requirements on how this should be displayed</a>, depending on how you are using our data.
For example, different rules apply on how to show the attribution notice depending on whether you have
created a browsable map, a printed map or a static image.
More details can be found in the <a href="https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Attribution_Guidelines">attribution guidelines</a> section of <a href="https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence">the licensing requirements</a>.

    </p>
    <p>
      Generally speaking,
to make clear that the data is available under the Open
Database License, you may link to <a href="/copyright">this copyright page</a>.
If you are distributing OSM in
data form, please name and link directly to the license(s). In media
where links are not possible (e.g. printed works),
please include the full URL on the page, e.g. https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright.

    </p>

      In this example, the credit appears in the corner of the map:

    </p>

    <h3>Copyright infringement</h3>
    <p>
      OSM contributors are reminded never to add data from any
copyrighted sources (e.g. Google Maps or printed maps) without
explicit permission from the copyright holders.

    </p>
    <p>
      If you believe that copyrighted material has been inappropriately
added to the OpenStreetMap database or this site, please refer
to our <a href="https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Takedown_procedure">takedown procedure</a> or file directly at our
<a href="https://dmca.openstreetmap.org/">on-line filing page</a>.

    </p>

    <p>
      OpenStreetMap, the magnifying glass logo, and State of the Map are registered trademarks of the
OpenStreetMap Foundation. If you have questions about your use of the marks, please see our
<a href="https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Trademark_Policy">Trademark Policy</a>.

    </p>

    <h3>Additional services</h3>
    <p>
      Although OpenStreetMap is open data, we cannot provide a
free-of-charge map API or map tiles for third-parties.
See our <a href="https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/api/">API Usage Policy</a>, <a href="https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/">Tile Usage Policy</a> and <a href="https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/">Nominatim Usage Policy</a>.

    </p>
