Mechanism: "oversized autosized airflow" (recognized in plan §R-B1.1)

→ E+ computes negative zone volume (-296.87 m³ and -65.99 m³) → forced to 10 m³.
This pathological volume causes E+ to autosize supply airflow far above physics:

  way/402307205 (254.5 m²):

    Autosized max supply air: ~5.4 m³/s  (physics-correct: ~0.24 m³/s)
    Design minimum (OA-based, E+ physics): 1.4% of max = 0.076 m³/s
    User-forced minimum (RESULT_02 30%):   0.30 × 5.4  = 1.62 m³/s
    → 21× the OA-required minimum

  way/402307206 (56.6 m²):
    Zone volume (computed): -65.99 m³ → forced 10 m³
    Autosized max supply air: 2.983 m³/s  (physics-correct: ~0.22 m³/s)
    Design minimum (OA-based): 1.4% = 0.043 m³/s
    User-forced minimum (30%): 0.30 × 2.983 = 0.895 m³/s
    → 21× the OA-required minimum
    Source: ComponentSizes SQL "Design Size Constant Minimum Air Flow Fraction = 0.014447"

  Cooling design load = 40,418 W for 56.6 m² = 714 W/m²  (typical: 50-80 W/m²)
  Mechanism: inverted surface normals cause E+ sizing engine to see enormous
  solar + conductive gains through the geometry-defect floor.

With 30% minimum forced on top of a 20-30× overautosized airflow:
